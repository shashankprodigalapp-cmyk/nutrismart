Food data

  • Indian Food Composition Tables 2017, ICMR–National Institute of Nutrition, Hyderabad (Longvah, Ananthan, Bhaskarachary and Venkaiah).
  • Indian Nutrient Databank (INDB), Jaacks et al., Current Developments in Nutrition, 2024 (DOI 10.1016/j.cdnut.2024.103790), built from the ICMR-NIN tables and other public sources.
  • McCance and Widdowson's Composition of Foods Integrated Dataset, UK Public Health England / OHID, used under the Open Government Licence v3.0.
  • USDA FoodData Central, US Department of Agriculture (public domain).

Nutrition values are estimates for typical portions and will differ by recipe and brand.

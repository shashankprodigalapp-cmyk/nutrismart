Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

NutriSmart collects food logs, energy check-ins, and kitchen profile data solely to provide personalised nutrition insights to you. We do not sell or share your personal data with advertisers or third parties.

Data stored

  • Email address (for account identification)
  • Food logs, water logs, energy check-ins
  • Kitchen multiplier profile
  • UPI transaction ID (for payment verification only)

Deletion

Email support to request full account and data deletion within 30 days.

Analytics

Aggregated, anonymised usage statistics are used to improve the app. No individual-level data is shared externally.

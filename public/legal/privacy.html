Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026. NutriSmart is a nutrition tracker for adults (18 and over). This page explains what we collect, why, who helps us process it, and your choices. Questions or complaints: support@nutrismart.co.in.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address and name (from Google sign-in or email sign-up).
  • What you log: foods, water, energy check-ins, your goals, and your kitchen profile.
  • Optional profile answers: age range, gender, state, main goal and diet. You can skip these. If you answer all five and agree, you get 5 extra AI searches a day. You can change or delete them at any time in Profile.
  • How the app is used: which screens and features you use, errors, and basic device details (phone type, language, app version, whether it is installed). We use this only in aggregate to fix and improve the app.
  • Notifications: if you turn them on, a device address used only to send you notifications.
  • Payments (only if you pay): your UPI transaction ID, to verify the payment. We do not see or store your card or bank details.

Photos and AI search

When you search with AI, the food name or description you type is sent to our AI providers (Google Gemini and Anthropic Claude) to work out the nutrition. When you use photo search, your photo is uploaded briefly so it can be analysed, and it is deleted straight after. We do not keep your photos. Do not include faces, documents or other people's information in photos.

Please note: we currently use Google's free developer service for Gemini. Under Google's terms for that service, Google may use what is sent to it to improve its products and a person may review it. We pass only the food name or the meal photo, with no name or email. We plan to move to Google's paid service, which does not use your content this way, and will update this page when we do.

We do not sell your data and we do not use advertising or third-party tracking tools.

Who can see your data

Your logs and answers are protected so that only your own account can read them. The person who runs NutriSmart sees totals and trends (for example "how many people logged this week"). Groups smaller than 10 people are not broken down. For managing accounts and subscriptions, the operator can see your name, email and plan.

Security

Data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and is encrypted at rest by our database provider. Access to administration is limited to the operator. No system is perfectly secure; if there is a breach that affects you, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

How long we keep it

We keep your account data until you delete your account. Technical logs are deleted automatically after 90 to 365 days. Your photos are deleted straight after analysis.

Your choices

  • Change or delete your profile answers in Profile.
  • Turn notifications off in your phone or browser settings.
  • Ask us to access, correct or delete your data, or withdraw consent, by emailing support@nutrismart.co.in. We will delete your account and data within 30 days.
  • Complaints: email the same address and we will reply within 7 days. If you are not satisfied, you may approach the Data Protection Board of India.

Children

NutriSmart is for adults only. If you are under 18, please do not use it.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will update the date above and tell you in the app.
